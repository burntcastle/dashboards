Methodology & Notes

Deflation: All real-terms figures are expressed in 2023/24 prices using the HM Treasury GDP deflator series. Financial years run April–March (e.g. "1985" = FY 1985/86).
Category definitions: "Personnel" includes military and civilian pay, pensions, and allowances. "Equipment & Support" covers procurement, equipment support, and research & development. "Infrastructure & Admin" includes estate, IT, and administrative costs. "Other" captures miscellaneous costs and war pensions.
Operations: Net Additional Costs of Military Operations (NACMO) are funded from the Treasury Reserve, outside the core defence budget. These are shown separately. Data before FY 2001/02 is estimated.
Personnel strength: Regular forces (trained and untrained). Reserve and volunteer forces are excluded. Civilian figures include trading funds (e.g. DSTL, DECA) where available.
Data quality: Category breakdowns for earlier years (pre-1990) use broader NATO-consistent categories and may not align exactly with later MOD accounting classifications. Figures are compiled from multiple official sources and may show minor discrepancies with any single publication due to rounding and definitional changes over the period.
